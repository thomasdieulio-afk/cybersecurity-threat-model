risks, document adversary profiles, enumerate attack vectors, and define a layered
set of mitigations and detection strategies for the target environment.

## Scope

The model covers:
- Identification and profiling of relevant threat actors
- Enumeration of the attack surface and exposed assets
- Deception strategy design (honeypots and canary tokens)
- Detection engineering and alerting logic
- Prioritized mitigation and control recommendations
